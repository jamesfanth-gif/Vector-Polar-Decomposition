Analytical Step-by-Step Cartesian ➔ Polar

Step 1: Calculate Modulus (Magnitude) $R$ Pythagorean
$$R = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$$
R = √((-8.20)² + (3.00)²) = √(67.24 + 9.00) = 8.73
Step 2: Reference Angle $\alpha$ (with nearest horizontal axis) Acute Triangle Angle
$$\alpha = \tan^{-1}\left(\frac{|y|}{|x|}\right)$$
α = tan⁻¹(|3.00| / |-8.20|) = tan⁻¹(0.3659) = 20.10°
Step 3: Standard Polar Angle $\theta$ (Counterclockwise from $+x$) Rule: 180° - α
$$\theta = 180^\circ - \alpha$$
θ = 180° - 20.10° = 159.90°
